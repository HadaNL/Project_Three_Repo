About the Game

This Way That Way was inspired by the ghost race from Ghost Carnival Project.
We took the idea of the race and made the race a 2-player game.
Additionally, a second level that requires teamwork was added to the project.

During development, I added zones around the map that are used to make the
boundaries. The zones help when I need to have something happen
in specific zones of the map.

The process started in Illustrator and, after completing a few zones, it was
continued in VS Code. The zones are instances of a class. This
class includes their position, color value, name, and opacity.

The last three images show the process and a small portion of code for the zones.

Besides coding, I also wanted to attempt to make pixel art.
I used a website called Pixilart, where I made the characters above.

I also worked on a sprite sheet, and after I was done I decided to have drawings
for all the different winning possibilities. Each character has 12 drawings,
but then I realized that in our game we allow mirror matches, and so I
made a few more drawings.
